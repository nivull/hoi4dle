# HOI4dle
A guessing game for Hearts of Iron 4



# General Guide

The obvious stuff:
Green = Correct/Exact
Yellow = Close (Explained below)

### Borders
> When yellow, your guess is **bordering the correct country**

### Ideology
> There isn't any yellow, just green (correct) or gray (incorrect)

### Factories
>Displays your guess, with an arrow pointing if the correct country has more or less factories

>Up arrow = correct country has more, and vise versa

>If the guess is yellow, that means you are within **10 factories**  of the correct amount

### Continennt
>Yellow if the guessed country's continent borders the correct country's continent, borders are listed below

>North America & South America

>West Asia: Europe, Africa, Asia

>Asia: West Asia, China

>China: Asia

>Europe: West Asia, Asia

>Africa: West Asia

>Oceania: None

West Asia, China, and the rest of Asia are split up because the countries are vastly too similar, and there are way too many (why did they even add so many useless Chinese warlords lmao)
